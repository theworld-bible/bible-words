Bible Words is an app that lets you read a Bible verse from the King James Version (KJV) for each of the 365 days of the year, free of charge. You can also enter a worry or concern and receive a verse in response, generate a wallpaper with that verse, make a morning declaration at the “Morning Gate,” and use an AI chat feature.

This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and how you can have it deleted.

1.Information We Collect

The app handles the following information:

  • Text you enter. The worries, concerns, or questions you type into the app when asking for a verse or using the AI chat.
  • Usage data stored in the app. Records created as you use the app, such as your Morning Gate declarations and your streak (consecutive-day) records.

The app does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name or address.

2.How We Use Information

The information above is used only for the following purposes:

  • To present a Bible verse in response to what you enter;
  • To generate a wallpaper containing that verse;
  • To generate replies in the AI chat; and
  • To keep your in-app records, such as Morning Gate declarations and streaks, working as features of the app.

We do not use this information for any other purpose.

3.Sharing with Third Parties

To provide the verse suggestions and AI chat replies, the text you enter may be sent to an external AI service for processing. This transmission is made solely so that the AI service can generate a response to return to you within the app.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

4.Data Storage and Deletion

In-app data — such as your Morning Gate declarations and streak records — is stored on your own device or in your browser, not on a server operated by us.

To delete your data, you can:

  • Clear the app’s data within the app or through your device or browser settings; or
  • Contact us at the email address in Section 7 below and ask for deletion.

Text sent to an external AI service for processing, as described in Section 3, is handled by that service under its own policies.

5.Children

Bible Words is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

6.Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will announce the changes on this page and update the “Last updated” date shown at the top.
